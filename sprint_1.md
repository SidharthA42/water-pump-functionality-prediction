# Sprint 1: Pump's Challenge

## Hypotheses

- Pump type influences functionality.
- The installer affects pump reliability.
- Geography impacts pump functionality.
- Pump age correlates with failure.
- Payment type affects maintenance.
- Larger population affects pump functionality.

## Research Questions

- Do older pumps fail more often?
- Are larger population areas more likely to have pump issues?
- Are certain regions or basins more likely to fail?
- How does the installer impact reliability?
- Which pump types are most functional?
- Does paying for water relate to better maintenance?
- How does water quality and quantity affect functionality?
- Does poor water quality lead to pump failure?
